We Have the Tech, Now What? Panel at next.app devCon 2026

November 13, 2026

Besides my Think Local talk, I joined the Friday keynote panel at next.app devCon 2026 in Berlin, together with Claire, Mihaela and Łukasz.

The panel started from a simple question. Foundation models and Siri-driven capabilities are now part of the Apple platforms, so how do teams at commercial companies turn them into real value? We talked about architecture trade-offs, privacy, and user experience, and about how to get past novelty features to things that actually matter to users.
