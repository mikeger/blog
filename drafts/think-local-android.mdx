On 16 October 2026 I gave the Android version of my Think Local talk at [KotlinLeeds](https://kotlinleeds.co.uk) in the Leeds Playhouse.

Most of the talk matches the iOS version, which I wrote up in detail in [Think Local: Magic at Your Fingertips](/posts/think-local): how a model runs on a phone, the lessons from shipping on-device TTS, and why I think AI is moving onto the device. The platform part is different. It covers why we picked ExecuTorch, why on Android you compile the model once per chip family on a build server and ship all of them in the APK, and the native library problems we had to solve before the first inference ran.
